Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Dubai and provide services all over the United Arab Emirates — from Dubai Marina and Palm Jumeirah to Abu Dhabi and Sharjah. Contact us to confirm coverage for your community.

Yes. Every booking starts with a free meet & greet so your pet can meet their carer and we can build a tailored care plan together — no obligation.

Will I receive updates about my pet?

Absolutely. You'll receive photo and video updates during every visit or walk, so you always know your pet is happy, fed and safe.
