Daily home visits from cat lovers who understand feline behaviour — your cat keeps its territory, routine and peace of mind.

Why Cat Sitting at Home Is Best for Most Cats

Cats are territorial animals. Their sense of security comes from familiar smells, favourite perches and predictable routines — remove those, and even confident cats can become stressed, stop eating or develop litter-tray issues. Our cat sitting Dubai service protects everything your cat loves: we visit your home once or twice a day while your cat stays exactly where it feels safest.

Every visit is carried out by a vetted, feline-experienced carer — never a rotating stranger. Your cat meets them first at a free meet & greet, and you receive photo updates after every visit, including those precious "actually, I do like the new human" moments.

What Every Cat Sitting Visit Includes

  • Feeding & hydration: your cat's exact food, portions and schedule; water bowls or fountains refreshed daily.
  • Litter hygiene: trays scooped every visit and fully changed as needed — a clean tray prevents most stress behaviours.
  • Play & enrichment: wand toys, puzzle feeders, brushing and window-perch company, matched to your cat's energy.
  • Medication support: tablets, liquids and topical treatments — including insulin and thyroid medication — per your vet's instructions.
  • Welfare check: appetite, eyes, coat and behaviour noted every visit, so small issues are caught early.
  • Photo & video report: sent to your phone after every single visit.

Shy, Senior & Special-Needs Cats Welcome

Hider? Hiss-er? Nineteen-year-old queen who rules the sofa? We've cared for them all. Our carers never force interaction — we complete every welfare task calmly and let your cat approach in its own time. For senior cats we add gentle health monitoring; for kittens, extra play and feeding visits as part of our wider pet sitting in Dubai service.

Cat Sitting or Cat Boarding?

For most cats — especially shy, elderly or territorial ones — home visits win. But if you're renovating, moving, or away for a long period, a quiet, cat-only stay may suit better: see our cat boarding in Dubai page for a full comparison. Frequently Asked Questions

How often do you visit my cat?

Most cats thrive with one or two visits per day. We tailor the frequency to your cat's age, health and personality at the free meet & greet.

My cat hides from strangers — will you still check on her?

Yes. We're experienced with shy cats: full welfare checks happen regardless, food, water and litter are refreshed, and your cat is never forced to interact.

Can you give my cat medication?

Yes — oral and topical medication, including insulin and thyroid tablets, administered exactly as your veterinarian prescribes.
