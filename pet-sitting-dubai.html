Pet Sitting in Dubai That Fits Your Schedule

Every pet — and every owner — is different. A weekend brunch, a two-week holiday and a month-long business trip all need different levels of care. Our pet sitting Dubai service is built around flexibility: you choose the visit style, frequency and duration, and your dedicated pet sitter in Dubai follows your written care plan to the letter.

Because we come to you, your pets never face car rides, strange noises or unfamiliar animals. Their food bowl stays in the same corner, their favourite sunny spot stays theirs, and their routine stays intact — the single biggest factor in preventing pet stress and separation anxiety.

Choose the Pet Sitting Style That Fits

  • Drop-in visits (30 or 60 minutes): feeding, fresh water, litter or garden clean-up, playtime and a photo report.
  • Multiple daily visits: ideal for puppies, senior pets, pets on medication or multi-pet homes.
  • Overnight house sitting: your sitter sleeps at your home, keeping evenings and mornings exactly as normal.
  • Extended holiday cover: consistent daily care for long trips, with the same familiar carer throughout.
  • Midday check-ins: a quick break, toilet stop and cuddle for pets whose owners work long hours.

A Typical Sitting Visit, Step by Step

  1. Arrival & welfare check: we greet your pet and check eyes, ears, appetite and mood.
  2. Feeding & hygiene: meals to your exact instructions, fresh water, clean litter or enclosure.
  3. Enrichment: play, grooming, cuddles — or a dog walk in Dubai for dogs who need exercise.
  4. Report card: photos, videos and a written summary sent to your phone before we leave.

Pet Sitting vs Boarding — Which Is Right for Your Pet?

As a rule of thumb, territorial or anxious pets (especially cats) do best with in-home cat sitting in Dubai, while confident, social dogs may love the playtime of dog boarding in Dubai. Not sure? We'll advise honestly at your free meet & greet — sometimes a mix works best. You can also read our partner's helpful overview of Pet Sitting Dubai for more tips on preparing your home and pets.
