Cat Boarding Dubai — Safe, Quiet & Comfortable Cat Stays

Cat-only boarding with private suites, calm routines, daily photo updates and round-the-clock supervision.

Reserve Your Cat's Stay

When Cat Boarding Makes Sense

Most cats prefer staying home — but not every situation allows it. If you're travelling for an extended period, renovating, moving apartments or having AC maintenance done (a Dubai classic!), a safe, quiet boarding stay is often the kindest option. Our cat boarding Dubai service is designed around one principle: cats need calm. No barking dogs, no crowded communal runs — just a clean, climate-controlled, cat-only environment with staff who understand feline body language.

Not sure whether boarding or home visits suit your cat better? Read our cat sitting in Dubai page, or ask us at your free consultation — we'll always recommend what's genuinely best for your cat.

What Your Cat's Stay Includes

  • Private suite: a comfortable individual space with resting shelves, hiding spots and scratch posts — no forced contact with other cats.
  • Your cat's own food: same brand, same portions, same mealtimes, to prevent tummy upsets and food refusal.
  • Twice-daily litter care: trays scooped morning and evening and kept spotless.
  • Play & attention: daily one-to-one sessions matched to your cat's confidence level.
  • Daily photo updates: pictures and notes sent to your phone every day of the stay.
  • 24/7 supervision: our team is on hand around the clock, every day of the week.

Health & Safety First

All guests must be up to date on core FVRCP vaccinations and flea treatment, and we ask for the vaccination record at check-in. Suites are cleaned and disinfected between guests, food and water bowls are sanitised daily, and a veterinarian is on call should anything ever need attention. Cats from the same household can share a suite if they prefer company; unrelated cats never mix.

What to Pack for Your Cat

  • Your cat's usual food for the full stay (plus a little extra)
  • A familiar blanket or unwashed t-shirt with your scent
  • Any medication, clearly labelled with vet instructions
  • The vaccination record and your vet's contact details

Frequently Asked Questions

What vaccinations does my cat need for boarding?

Core FVRCP vaccinations and current flea treatment are required. Please bring the vaccination record to check-in — it keeps every guest safe.

Can you feed prescription diets and give medication?

Yes. We feed your cat's own food, including prescription diets, and administer medication exactly as your veterinarian instructs.

Can I see the boarding setup before booking?

Absolutely. Contact us to arrange a viewing or free meet & greet — we want you to feel completely confident before your cat's stay.
