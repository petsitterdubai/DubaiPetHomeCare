Why Choose Our Pet Sitter Dubai Service?

Leaving your pets behind when you travel or work long hours is stressful — for you and for them. With a professional pet sitter in Dubai from Dubai Pet Home Care, your pets stay in the one place they feel safest: home. Their bed, their smells, their routine — all unchanged, while a trusted carer handles everything from feeding to affection.

Unlike busy kennels, in-home care removes transport stress, exposure to unfamiliar animals and the anxiety of strange environments. Every booking begins with a free meet & greet, where your dedicated sitter learns your pet's feeding schedule, quirks, favourite games and medical needs. Whether you need flexible pet sitting in Dubai or a dedicated dog sitter in Dubai, we match the right carer to your pet — not the other way around.

What's Included in Every Pet Sitting Visit

  • Feeding & fresh water: exact portions, brands and schedules you specify — including raw or prescription diets.
  • Medication administration: oral and topical medication given safely, following your vet's instructions.
  • Playtime & exercise: age-appropriate enrichment, plus optional dog walking in Dubai for active dogs.
  • Clean living spaces: litter trays, cages, enclosures and feeding areas cleaned every visit.
  • Photo & video updates: a report after every visit so you never have to wonder how your pet is doing.
  • Home care extras: watering plants, collecting mail and rotating lights for added security while you're away.
