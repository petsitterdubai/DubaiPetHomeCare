About Dubai Pet Home Care

The people behind the paws — a Dubai-based team caring for pets like family, 24 hours a day, all over the UAE.

Our Story

Dubai Pet Home Care began with a simple frustration shared by pet parents across the UAE: travelling for work or family meant worrying about the pets left behind. Kennels felt impersonal, favours from friends ran out, and "pet-friendly" solutions rarely respected a pet's actual routine.

So we built the service we wanted ourselves — professional carers who come to your home, follow your instructions to the letter, and treat your pet's routine as sacred. Today we are based in Dubai and provide services all over United Arab Emirates, from pet sitting in Dubai and dog walking to quiet cat boarding and home-style dog boarding — all under one promise: your pet's comfort comes first.

What We Stand For

Compassion

Our Care Promise

Whether it's a 30-minute drop-in or a three-week boarding stay, every Dubai Pet Home Care booking follows the same standard:

  • A free meet & greet before any new booking — no exceptions
  • One dedicated carer who knows your pet, plus a trained backup
  • Your written care plan followed exactly: food, routine, rules and medication
  • Updates after every visit or walk — photos, videos and honest notes
  • Immediate contact if anything seems off, with a veterinarian on call
