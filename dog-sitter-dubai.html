Meet Your Dog's New Sitter

What Makes a Great Dog Sitter?

Anyone can fill a food bowl. A great dog sitter in Dubai reads canine body language, spots stress before it becomes a problem, handles a strong puller safely, keeps training rules consistent, and notices when something's "off" with your dog's health. That's the standard we hire to: every Dubai Pet Home Care sitter is background-checked, experienced with dogs of all sizes and temperaments, and trained in pet first aid basics.

Just as importantly, we match personality. A bouncy Labrador needs a different human than a timid rescue greyhound. That's why every booking starts with a free meet & greet — if the match isn't right, we introduce another sitter. No awkwardness, no obligation.

What Our Dog Sitters Do

  • Companionship: calm presence, cuddles and play — the antidote to lonely, destructive afternoons.
  • Feeding & medication: your exact portions, schedules and vet instructions, every time.
  • Exercise: heat-safe dog walking in Dubai plus garden and enrichment games.
  • Training reinforcement: we uphold your rules — sit before meals, no sofa, loose-lead walking.
  • Puppy & senior care: frequent toilet breaks for pups; gentle pacing and health monitoring for old souls.
  • Updates: photos, videos and honest notes after every visit.

How We Match You With the Right Sitter

  1. Tell us about your dog: breed, age, energy, quirks, medical needs and handling rules.
  2. Free meet & greet: your matched sitter visits, walks your dog and builds a written care plan.
  3. Backup included: a second trained carer also meets your dog, so illness never leaves you stranded.

Working Late? Midday Drop-Ins Help

Long office days are hard on social dogs. A midday drop-in from your regular sitter breaks the loneliness, covers the toilet stop and burns energy before you get home. Combine it with our full dog sitting in Dubai service when you travel, or browse our partner's resource on Dog Sitter Dubai for more tips on choosing a carer you can trust.
