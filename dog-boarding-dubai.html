Comfy beds, supervised play, daily walks and round-the-clock attention — a holiday for your dog while you take yours.

Boarding That Feels Like Home, Not a Kennel

Traditional kennels can be loud, cold and lonely. Our dog boarding Dubai service is the opposite: a home-style environment where dogs sleep comfortably, play in small, temperament-matched groups (or enjoy solo care if they prefer), and receive genuine one-to-one attention every day. For social, confident dogs, it's honestly a holiday — new friends, new sniffs, and humans who throw the ball all day.

If your dog is anxious in new places or prefers its own territory, we'll tell you honestly — in-home dog sitting in Dubai may be the better fit. The right choice always depends on the dog, not the booking.

A Day in the Life of a Boarding Guest

  1. Morning: a fresh-air walk at the coolest time of day, then breakfast — your food, your portions.
  2. Midday: supervised play in matched groups, enrichment games, then a proper nap (dogs need 14+ hours of sleep!).
  3. Afternoon: one-to-one attention, brushing, training games and hydration checks.
  4. Evening: second walk, dinner, wind-down cuddles and a comfortable night's sleep indoors.
  5. Every day: photos and a written summary sent to your phone.

Health, Safety & Requirements

All guests must be current on core DHPP vaccinations, kennel cough (Bordetella) and flea/tick prevention — bring the record to check-in. Every dog has a short temperament assessment before joining any group play, and groups are kept small and supervised at all times. In Dubai's summer, outdoor play is limited to early morning and evening, with air-conditioned indoor enrichment during the heat of the day. A veterinarian is on call, and our team is present 24 hours a day.

What to Bring

  • Your dog's usual food for the whole stay, pre-portioned if possible
  • Lead, collar or harness with ID tag
  • A familiar blanket or toy for comfort
  • Medication with written vet instructions, plus the vaccination record

Frequently Asked Questions

What vaccinations are required for dog boarding?

Core DHPP, kennel cough (Bordetella) and current flea/tick prevention. Bring the vaccination record to check-in — it protects every guest.

My dog doesn't like other dogs — can he still board?

Yes. We offer solo boarding with one-to-one walks and play, so dog-selective guests enjoy a safe, happy stay without group contact.

Will I get updates during my dog's stay?

Yes — daily photos plus a written summary of meals, walks, play and mood, every single day.
