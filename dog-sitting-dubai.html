Dog Sitting Dubai — In-Home Dog Care While You're Away

Feeding, walks, play and overnight companionship in your dog's own home — so the only thing they notice is how fun the sitter is.

Book a Free Meet & Greet

Why Dogs Thrive With In-Home Sitting

Dogs are creatures of routine. The morning walk, the food bowl at six, the sofa cuddle at nine — break those, and many dogs become anxious, refuse meals or act out. Our dog sitting Dubai service keeps every part of your dog's day exactly as it should be, in the home they know best, while you travel or work.

Your dedicated dog sitter in Dubai is matched at a free meet & greet and follows a written care plan covering meals, walks, training rules (yes, we enforce "no sofa" if you do!) and bedtime habits. It's the closest thing to you never having left.

What's Included in Dog Sitting

  • Feeding to your schedule: exact portions and diets, including raw feeding and food-topping routines.
  • Walks & toilet breaks: heat-safe dog walking in Dubai built into every care plan.
  • Play & companionship: fetch, tug, enrichment games and plenty of affection — boredom is the root of most bad behaviour.
  • Medication & health checks: tablets, ear drops and supplements per your vet's instructions, with daily wellbeing notes.
  • Overnight option: your sitter sleeps at your home so your dog is never alone at night.
  • Daily photo & video updates: walk reports, meal confirmations and adorable proof of happiness.

Puppies, Seniors & Anxious Dogs

Puppies need frequent toilet breaks and routine reinforcement; seniors need gentle exercise and closer health monitoring; anxious or rescue dogs need patience and a calm, predictable human. We tailor visit frequency and style to all three — and we're honest if we think your dog would actually enjoy dog boarding in Dubai with social playtime more. Your dog's wellbeing always beats a booking.

Dog Sitting vs Dog Boarding

Choose sitting if your dog is territorial, anxious in new places, unvaccinated for boarding, or simply happiest at home. Choose boarding if your dog is confident, social and loves other dogs. Either way you get the same 24/7 support and daily updates. For more preparation tips, our partner's guide to Dog Sitting Dubai is a great read.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer overnight dog sitting?

Yes. Your sitter stays overnight at your home, keeping evening and morning routines intact — ideal for dogs with separation anxiety.

Are walks included in dog sitting?

Yes. Walks or garden breaks are built into your care plan, always following our heat-safe summer walking policy.

Can you look after two or more dogs?

Absolutely. Multi-dog households are welcome — each dog gets its own feeding, walking and medication routine.
